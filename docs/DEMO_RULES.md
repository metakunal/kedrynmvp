# Demo rules and data notes

- Fixed clock: 2026-10-02 10:00 IST. Delivery dates: October 3–9. Serviceable sample PINs: 560001 and 560038.
- Breakfast 08:00–10:00, lunch 12:00–14:00, snacks 16:00–18:00, dinner 19:00–21:00 IST.
- Each date/window is a dispatch group. ₹40 delivery is waived at ₹500 merchandise per group. No merchandise discount or added tax. These are invented assumptions.
- Money is stored in paise. Flexible portion unit price is rounded half-up once, then multiplied by quantity. Commission is 10% of merchandise after discounts, rounded half-up once per order. Delivery and tax are excluded.
- Nutrition scales from the standard recipe estimate. Calories are an independent estimate; rounded macro values need not reconcile exactly with a 4/4/9 calculation. Aggregates use unrounded values before display.
- Ingredient and allergen exclusions use explicitly stated fixture tokens and aliases. This cannot establish cross-contact safety. M15 is unavailable throughout the schedule; M18 is unavailable for dinner on October 4.
- Purchased food is logged only through an explicit confirmation. Photos are not recognized; bundled examples have predetermined results, and uploads receive editable generic values.
- The stated acceptance figure of ₹2,730 eligible seed sales conflicts with the specified lines and prices. Those lines sum to ₹2,728 (498+418+438+458+398+518). The stated ₹272.80 commission agrees with ₹2,728 at 10%. The app uses calculated ₹2,728, preserving all supplied prices and commission states.
