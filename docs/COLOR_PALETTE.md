The reference's burgundy and warm neutral relationship carries through the masthead, calls to action, page surfaces and generated referral artwork. Terracotta replaces high-saturation chart accents. Text and control contrast should be checked in context; the interface keeps primary text dark on light surfaces and light on burgundy surfaces.
